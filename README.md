## 👨‍💻 About me

I build reliable backend systems for fintechs. I combine Java expertise with domain knowledge in **cards, payment processing, fraud prevention and chargebacks**, working within a software architecture team to connect business requirements with technical decisions.

I care about reliability, maintainability and clear integration boundaries, and I believe software should be understandable and adaptable for the engineers who maintain it, not only meet today's requirements.

My main focus is backend, complemented by **mobile and frontend** experience. That broader view helps me see how customer-facing apps, backend services and payment processors interact, and how a decision in one layer affects the whole solution.
